## Dependency notes

- 001 comes first because it adds `tools/smoke.mjs`, the one-command browser check that every later plan uses as a gate.
- 002, 004, 006, 008, 009 and 010 change the simulation. Each one must bump `SIM_VERSION` in `src/sim/race.js`, re-bake the author ghosts with `node tools/medals.mjs all`, and leave the Worker redeploy to the owner. The save key contains the sim version (`src/main.js:37`), so every bump clears local records. The game has not been announced yet, so land these before the launch posts go out.
- 004 and 006 follow 002 because they edit the same functions in `src/sim/race.js` and `src/sim/drive.js`.
- 009 follows 002 because 002 changes how near misses and landings pay, and 009 tunes the amounts on top.
- 011 follows 009 because track changes should be judged with the final gas economy.
- 010 and 011 are design spikes. They end with a written proposal and a prototype branch, not a merge.

## One-way doors that stay with the owner

No executor may do these. Each plan repeats the ones that apply.

- `npx wrangler deploy` in `server/` (the live Worker must match the game's `SIM_VERSION`, or every submitted run is rejected with 422).
- Deleting rows from the D1 `voltitude` database (runs recorded on an older sim version stay on the board with times the new physics cannot reproduce).
- `git push`, and anything that triggers the Vercel deploy.

## Evidence the plans rest on

- A scripted cold session and five autopilot runs in headless Chrome on an Apple M4 Pro, 1280x720, taken from `git archive 9ee19cc`.
- Four read-only code audits (driving feel, session flow and HUD, race design and AI, audio and effects). Every cited line was re-read by the advisor.
- Measured facts quoted in the plans:
  - Proving Ground Time Trial, throttle held from GO: 148 mph at 7 s, then a tyre wall. 0 mph for 2 s with no toast before the auto-reset.
  - Proving Ground Grand Prix on autopilot: 4:20.7, airborne 6.4% of samples, boosting 5.5%, longest gap between toasts 44 s.
  - The Lineup on autopilot: clears at 11.2, 47.9, 84.2 and 121.2 s.
  - Enter to GO on the first race of a session: 5.5 s for a 2 s countdown, with one 2.4 s frame (machine under load). Restart to GO: 2.0 s.
  - Frame times once racing, Launch Complex at night: p50 16.7 ms, p99 16.8 ms.

## Findings without a plan

- A second and third camera (far chase, bumper, look-back). `src/input.js:1-12` has no camera action. Worth doing after 003 settles the default camera.
- Golden-hour haze hides the far road (`src/render/post.js:284-292`, `:407`). 005 adds braking boards, which covers the worst case. The fog itself is an art-direction call.
- Touch controls on circuits: binary throttle, no reset button, and a resting thumb can hop (`src/touch.js:10`, `:64`). Needs a real phone before it is specified.
- A three-venue cup. Results print "Winner · 25 points" (`src/main.js:485`, `:554`) and nothing stores them. Wait for 008, since a cup of four-minute races is the wrong shape.
- A daily streak and stored daily rank (`src/main.js:272`, `:519`), and a daily modifier (`src/sim/daily.js`).
- Sparks, tyre smoke and tarmac landing dust are in 007 only as a single spark and dust pass. Car damage is not planned.

## Findings considered and rejected

- Hit-pause or slow motion on impacts: `docs/feel.md` decides against it because the sim is fixed at 120 Hz and a pause freezes the whole field.
- Rubber-banding for rivals: decided against in the design. Plan 002 adds a slower fixed tier instead.
- Passive gas refill: decided against. Plan 009 raises earned income instead.
- "Rivals spread out and the race becomes a time trial": not true. The nearest rival was within 25 m for 45% of a Proving race and 95% of a Launch race.
- "The frame rate is poor": not true once racing. Only the first-start freeze is open, in 012.
- "The camera clips through trench walls": a guess from code. Captured trench frames show no clipping.
- Canyon pass-by sounds may click (`src/audio.js` `passBy`): unverified by ear, low value.
