## Form language

- Faceted hard surface, closer to Cybertruck than to a curved supercar. Large planar panels meet at crisp creases.
- The front fenders are the only strongly convex surfaces. They crown above the wheels and fall to a crisp crease.
- The glass is one continuous canopy: windshield, three roof panes and the rear glass. In plan it narrows from 1.63 m at the A-pillar to 0.53 m at the tail. The side windows end in a sharp point behind the doors.
- The side has two planes: a bright up-facing shoulder band below the glass, and a near-vertical door plane below a crisp crease.
- The lower body is satin black. It is thin at the front wheel and sweeps up behind the rear wheel to tail-light height.
- The tail is a thin blade that carries a full-width red light bar and overhangs the black diffuser.
- The diffuser has four thruster nozzles (polished rings) above four vertical strakes. There is a plate reading "RDSTR".
- The rear wing has swan-neck mounts and endplates when deployed. It lies flush with the deck when stowed.
- At the nose, a full-width white LED strip runs along the top edge of the front face. Below it is a black intake and splitter.

## Wheels

- Six double spokes in gloss black over a flat light-grey aero disc, with a dark centre cap.
- Low-profile tyres. Measured radius: front 0.339 m, rear 0.369 m (config: 0.347 m and 0.364 m).
